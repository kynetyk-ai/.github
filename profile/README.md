# Kynetyk

**Humans × AI.** We write about what's actually happening in AI, build products that put humans and AI on the same team, and help companies figure out how to make AI genuinely useful.

We're not AI optimists or AI pessimists. We're AI realists.

→ [www.kynetyk.ai](https://www.kynetyk.ai)

## Studio

Products and experiences that reimagine collaborative work and play with humans and AI.

### [House Rules](https://house-rules.app)
Tabletop games for humans, friends, and AI. A free-form game platform where your group can play together via the web. **Beta is live.**

### [Jamb for Mac](https://getjamb.app)
Where your AI co-work goes to live. A local-first canvas on your Mac that holds reference material, dashboards, and the things you build with AI. Your AI knows how to build for it — and works in it directly.
