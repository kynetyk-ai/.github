
## What we do

*We believe there's a gap between what AI can do and what actually does. We help companies close it* 

We're an AI-forward consultancy built by leaders and operators in the healthcare and life sciences industry with experience implementing AI across the biotech organizations we ran. Since then we've helped similar companies to develop their AI implementation and AI product strategies.  The name comes from *kinetic* — the energy of things in motion. We believe that AI can be most valuable to the industries we serve when it's moving alongside people, not replacing them or operating in isolation.
